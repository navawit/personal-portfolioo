01 A little about me

Creative by nature.
Curious by design.

I’m Navawit, a multidisciplinary creative drawn to the space where visual storytelling, design, and technology meet.

From shaping a graphic to building an interactive experience, I enjoy turning early ideas into thoughtful work people can feel and use. I bring an eye for composition together with a hands-on approach to making — across the frame, the screen, and everything in between.
